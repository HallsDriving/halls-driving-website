HALL'S DRIVING — EMAIL AND FORMS LAUNCH SETUP

The Contact and Defensive Driving forms are configured for Netlify Forms and a Netlify Function.

APPROVED EMAIL FLOW
1. Contact form submission:
   - Hall's Driving receives the inquiry at Halls.driving@gmail.com.
   - The person submitting receives a branded confirmation stating that someone from the Hall's Driving family will respond within 24–48 hours.
2. Defensive Driving registration:
   - Hall's Driving receives the registration at Halls.driving@gmail.com.
   - The registrant receives a branded confirmation thanking them for registering for a Hall's Driving Defensive Driving class.

No scheduled reminder or post-class email is active in this launch version.

NETLIFY EMAIL SETUP
- Add RESEND_API_KEY in Netlify environment variables.
- Add OWNER_EMAIL = Halls.driving@gmail.com
- Add FROM_EMAIL using a sender address verified for HallsDrivingAL.com in Resend.
- Redeploy the site after adding variables.

IMPORTANT
Without RESEND_API_KEY, Netlify Forms can still collect submissions, but the branded owner notification and automatic customer email will not be sent by the function.

TEST BEFORE LAUNCH
- Submit the Contact form with a test email.
- Confirm Hall's Driving receives the inquiry.
- Confirm the test customer receives the 24–48 hour Hall's Driving family response.
- Submit a Defensive Driving registration.
- Confirm Hall's Driving receives the registration.
- Confirm the registrant receives the registration confirmation.
