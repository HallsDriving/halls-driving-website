HALL'S DRIVING EMAIL AND FORMS SETUP

The Contact and Defensive Driving forms are configured for Netlify Forms.
They also call a Netlify Function that can send:
1. A branded notification to Hall's Driving.
2. A branded automatic response to the person who submits the form.

To turn on branded email delivery in Netlify:
- Add environment variable RESEND_API_KEY.
- Add environment variable OWNER_EMAIL = Halls.driving@gmail.com
- Add environment variable FROM_EMAIL using a verified sender on HallsDrivingAL.com.
- Redeploy the site.

Without those email variables, Netlify can still collect form submissions after Forms are enabled, but branded automatic emails will not send.

POST-CLASS THANK-YOU EMAIL
The approved email is in email-templates/DEFENSIVE-DRIVING-POST-CLASS-THANK-YOU.txt.
Automatic delivery one hour after class requires a scheduling service or workflow to be connected after the live site and email sender are verified. It is not represented as active until that connection is completed and tested.

TEST BEFORE ANNOUNCING THE SITE
- Submit the Contact form using a test email.
- Confirm Hall's Driving receives the owner notification.
- Confirm the test customer receives the friendly auto-response.
- Submit a Defensive Driving registration and repeat the test.
- Confirm the logo displays in both emails.
